-Morning routine:

  1. Get out of bed and check my school schedule for the day.
  2. Open the curtains and let some natural light into my room.
  3. Wash up and get dressed for school.
  4. Pack my schoolbag and double-check that I have everything I need.
  5. Eat breakfast while mentally preparing for the day ahead.
  6. Review an important note or formula if I have a test or a difficult lesson.
  7. Leave home on time and head to school.

-Tips for studying:

  • Start with the subject you find most challenging while your mind is still fresh.
  • Try to understand the lesson instead of memorizing every sentence.
  • Break large assignments into smaller tasks so they feel easier to manage.
  • Keep a list of questions you want to ask your teachers.
  • Test yourself without looking at your notes to see what you actually remember.
  • Take short breaks when you notice your concentration fading.
  • Review difficult topics regularly instead of waiting until exam week.
